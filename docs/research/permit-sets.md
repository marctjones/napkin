## 1. Bloomfield, CT

Source: https://bloomfieldct.gov/187/Residential-Construction-Resources
("Residential Construction Resources", Bloomfield CT — retrieved 2026-09-26)

Linked documents (DocumentCenter IDs on bloomfieldct.gov):
- Permit Checklist (PDF) — https://bloomfieldct.gov/DocumentCenter/View/241 — titled
  "NEW HOUSE AND ADDITION CHECKLIST" (dated "RAN 4-1-08" at foot)
- Deck Requirements (PDF) — https://bloomfieldct.gov/DocumentCenter/View/240 — titled
  "REQUIRED FRAMING INFORMATION FOR RESIDENTIAL DECK PLANS" (dated "RAN 4/2/08")
- Structural Reminder (PDF) — https://bloomfieldct.gov/DocumentCenter/View/238 (not fetched
  in full text this pass; listed on the resources page)

### New House and Addition Checklist (View/241), full body as extracted by pdftotext:
Items checked off: certified plot plan; zoning approval; wetlands approval; engineering
approval; Health Dept sign-off on septic (if applicable); Health Dept sign-off on well (if
applicable); MDC approval for water/sewer connection (if applicable); building permit
application form; **"Complete set of legible building plans"**; **"Engineering details for
structural elements such as floors, headers, and roof trusses"**; contractor registration
certificate; workers' comp insurance form; ResCheck energy compliance form or proof on
plans; driveway excavation permit & fee (town or state).
→ This is Bloomfield's general checklist (additions/new house), not deck- or
window-specific, but a window/door enlargement in an exterior wall (a wall alteration) would
fall under it: a certified plot plan plus a complete, legible plan set with structural
engineering details.

### Deck Requirements (View/240), full body:
Framed as a fill-in worksheet, "REQUIRED FRAMING INFORMATION FOR RESIDENTIAL DECK PLANS":
- General: dimensions of the deck; how deck attaches to house; whether any part of the deck
  floor is >30" above grade.
- Floor joist: size, length (ft), spacing (inches o.c.).
- Beams: number of beams; size (e.g. "three 2x8's").
- Piers and posts: post size holding beams (posts must attach to beam with brackets or
  bracing — noted directly on the form); spacing between posts/piers (ft); pier size
  (inches); pier depth (inches).
- Flooring/stairs: decking material type; whether there is a stairway (stairways of 3+ steps
  require a graspable handrail).
- Guards/railings: whether a guard/railing exists (required if deck/stairway >30" above
  grade); guard height (inches); baluster/rail spacing (max 4").

No explicit mention on this Bloomfield form of scale, paper size, copy count, licensed
design professional, or a required plot plan specifically for decks — those are only stated
in the general checklist (plot plan) above. Bloomfield's page (per the earlier page-level
summary) also states that for projects other than interior work a certified plot plan must
be submitted, and zoning/engineering/health/fire marshal review and approval is required
before construction on such projects, but I could not independently re-verify that exact
sentence's source page beyond the initial page fetch of
https://bloomfieldct.gov/187/Residential-Construction-Resources — flagging as
lower-confidence paraphrase from that fetch, not a direct quote from a document I re-read.

## 2. Simsbury, CT

Source: "DECK GUIDELINES" PDF —
https://ct-simsbury.civicplus.com/DocumentCenter/View/1315/Deck-Guide (also mirrored at
simsbury-ct.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1315/Deck-Guide), linked from
https://www.simsbury-ct.gov/590/Building-Permit-GuidesInformation. Retrieved and read via
pdftotext 2026-09-26.

"Permit Application" section, verbatim structure:
1. Building plans for deck including: proposed deck size; height of structure from grade;
   size and spacing of floor joists; size, type, location and spacing of posts; size and
   type of beams; **size and depth of footings (minimum 42 inches deep for frost
   protection and minimum of 8 inches around)**; ledger attachment details and flashing
   information; size and type of decking material.
2. **Plot plan showing deck scaled with stairs.**
3. Building Permit Application signed by owner or licensed contractor, fee paid ($14.26 per
   $1,000 project cost, per this document — note the town's separate fee schedule page may
   have since updated this rate).
4. Zoning Compliance Form ($25 fee).
5. Approval from Farmington Valley Health District (septic) or WPCA (sewer), if applicable.

The rest of the document reproduces 2015 IRC deck tables/requirements (not town-specific):
drilling/notching allowances, hardware corrosion-resistance rules, guard height (36" min
above deck floor; 34" min on open stair sides), guard opening limits (4" sphere; 4-3/8" at
stair treads; 6" at stair triangular openings), stair riser (8-1/4" max) and tread (9" min)
dimensions, stairway width (31-1/2" one handrail / 27" two handrails / 36" above handrail
height), handrail height (34"-38"). These are code citations reproduced by the town, not
independent town rules — noted as "Above tables from IRC2015" in the source document.

No scale/paper-size spec found in this document. No explicit copy-count for deck submittals. A
plot plan is required; no explicit statement of when a licensed design professional is required
beyond the general "signed by owner or licensed contractor" line.

## Synthesis

### Recurring drawing sheets

**For decks**, both towns' deck documents (Bloomfield's framing worksheet, Simsbury's Deck
Guidelines) ask for:

1. **A plot plan** showing the deck on the lot: Simsbury's "plot plan showing deck scaled with
   stairs"; Bloomfield's general checklist asks for a certified plot plan for any non-interior
   work.
2. **A framing plan**: deck size; joist size and spacing; beam size and number; post size and
   spacing; decking material — Bloomfield's worksheet and Simsbury's building-plans list.
3. **Elevation and detail information**: height above grade (Simsbury; Bloomfield asks whether
   any part is over 30" above grade); footing size and depth (Simsbury: 42" minimum for frost;
   Bloomfield: pier size and depth); ledger attachment and flashing (Simsbury); guard height and
   baluster spacing and a stair's handrail (Bloomfield's worksheet; Simsbury reproduces the IRC
   2015 guard and stair provisions).

Neither town cites AWC DCA 6 by name; Simsbury's document reproduces 2015 IRC deck tables as its
technical backing.

**For enlarging or relocating a window or door in an exterior wall**, neither town publishes a
window/door-specific drawing checklist. Bloomfield's general "New House and Addition Checklist"
would apply to any exterior-wall alteration: a certified plot plan, a "complete set of legible
building plans" and "engineering details for structural elements such as floors, headers, and
roof trusses" — for a window or door enlargement, the header is the structural element the town
expects to see.

### What neither town states

- **Copy count**: neither document read states one.
- **Scale or paper size**: neither states a scale or a sheet size.
- **Licensed design professional**: neither deck document states a trigger; Bloomfield's
  checklist implies engineering for the structural elements it lists, and asks for a *certified*
  plot plan.
