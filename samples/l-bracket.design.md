# L-bracket, asymmetric — the design, as you would write it on paper

An L-shaped bracket with one distinct feature on every side, so that the top, bottom, left, right, front and back views can each be told from all the others (needed by the standard-views work).

- top: the boss at the foot's south-east corner
- bottom: the skid under the foot
- east: the tab beyond the foot
- west: the nub on the upright
- south (front): the lug, high on the upright
- north (back): the rib, lower on the upright
