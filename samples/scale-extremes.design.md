# Scale extremes — the design, as you would write it on paper

A 40'-0" plate, a 96" wall standing on it, and a 1/32"-thick shim between: sizes that span 3.5 orders of magnitude, to test precision (1/32" is 32 units, a 40' length is 491520) and zoom. The wall is a thing built, not a part, so it has no cut-list row.

## Stated dimensions

| What | Dimension |
|---|---|
| Plate | 40'-0" x 3 1/2" x 1 1/2" |
| Shim | 6" x 3 1/2" x 1/32", on the plate at mid-span |
| Wall | 40'-0" x 3 1/2" x 96", on the shim (no part) |

Overall 40'-0" x 3 1/2" x 8'-1 17/32" (read as 8'-1 9/16", approximate). Two pieces, two rows. Derivations are in `scale-extremes.expected.json`.
